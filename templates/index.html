About Us

Built for the
Modern Scholar

Founded in 2024, pioneered AI-based academic management in the region. Our platform uses advanced facial recognition to automate attendance, auto-grade student tasks, and deliver real-time performance analytics to staff and administrators.

Biometric Attendance Students mark attendance automatically on face-recognition login — no manual registers needed.
Auto-Graded Tasks Activity task answers are graded instantly using keyword extraction algorithms for partial marking.
Live Analytics Department-wise attendance trends, score distributions, and performance charts — updated in real time.
